Delete your account

You can delete your Freehold account and everything we hold about it, at any time, from inside the app.

In the app

  1. Open Freehold
  2. Tap Settings
  3. Scroll to Account
  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm

It takes effect immediately.

What gets deleted

Everything we hold, which is a short list:

  • Your display name
  • Your account identifier
  • Your seat in any room you are in

There is no database behind the game. Rooms live in the server's memory and are gone when the room closes. So deletion is genuinely complete — there is nothing left over anywhere.

If you are in the middle of a game when you delete, a computer player finishes your hand so the game is not spoiled for everyone else. Your name is removed from it immediately.

Data kept afterwards

None. No backups, no archives, no analytics record. Nothing is retained.

Cannot reach the app?

If you have uninstalled it and want to be certain, email akashkalala123@gmail.com and we will confirm. Because nothing is stored on our side once a room closes, in almost every case there will already be nothing to delete.
