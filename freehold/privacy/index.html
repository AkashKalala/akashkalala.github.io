Privacy Policy — Freehold

Last updated: 26 August 2026

The short version

Freehold collects almost nothing.

  • No account is needed to play offline.
  • To play online you give us a display name. That is all we ask for.
  • We have no database. Nothing about you is written to disk on our servers.
  • We show no ads, and there are no analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anybody.

What we collect, and why

If you only play offline

Nothing at all leaves your device. The game runs entirely on your phone. The app

does not even open a connection to our server unless you go to the online screens or

have signed in before.

Your settings — sound on or off, vibration on or off, your name — are stored on your

own device and never sent anywhere.

If you play online

WhatWhyWhere it lives
Display nameSo other players can see whose turn it isIn memory on the server, only while you are in a room
An account idSo you can rejoin if your connection dropsIn memory, and inside a token on your own device
Your game stateIt is the gameIn memory on the server, until the room is closed
IP addressUnavoidable — it is how the internet worksHeld transiently by the server and its host to route your connection

We have no database. Everything above lives in the server's memory and is gone when

the room closes or the server restarts. There is no user table, no history, and no

record that you ever played.

If you sign in with Google or Apple

If those options are available in your build and you use one:

  • The provider tells us your account identifier and, with Google, your **display

name and profile picture URL**.

  • We use them to show your name to other players.
  • We never see your password. We never receive one. We ask Google or Apple to prove

who you are and check their signature.

  • We do not receive or store your email address.

Guest sign-in

A guest account is a random identifier with no personal information attached at all. It

lives only in a token on your device, and is gone if you reinstall the app.

What we do NOT do

  • ❌ No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • ❌ No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • ❌ No tracking across other apps or websites
  • ❌ No selling or sharing of anything, with anybody
  • ❌ No profiling, and no automated decisions about you
  • ❌ No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos — the app requests none of

these permissions, and blocks several explicitly

Crash reporting

The app contains crash-reporting code that is switched off in this build — no

reporting service is configured, so nothing is sent.

If it is ever turned on, this is how it will behave, and the Settings screen will say so

honestly:

  • Stack traces and device model only
  • Never your name, your account id, or what you played
  • On by default, with a switch in Settings → Privacy that takes effect immediately

rather than at the next launch

If that changes, this policy changes with it, before the build ships.

Children

Freehold is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect

personal information from children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, and

nothing to buy.

Your rights

Because we hold so little, most of these are simple:

  • See what we hold — your display name, and your current game. That is the complete

list.

  • Delete it — Settings → Account → Delete account, inside the app. It removes

your seat, your name, and everything the server holds about you, immediately. There is

no database behind it, so there is genuinely nothing left over.

  • Stop entirely — uninstall the app. Nothing about you persists on our side.

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you also have rights to rectification, restriction,

portability and objection. Given what is held — a display name, in memory, while you are

in a room — these are satisfied by changing your name or leaving the room.

Legal basis for processing (UK/EU GDPR): performing the contract you asked for,

namely running a game of cards you chose to join.

Where your data goes

The game server may be hosted outside your country. It processes only what is in the

table above, in memory, for the duration of a game.

Third parties involved, and only if you use them:

  • Google or Apple, if you choose to sign in with them, under their own privacy

policies.

There are no others. No ad networks, no analytics vendors, no data brokers.

Security

  • All connections use TLS in production (wss://).
  • Session tokens are signed JWTs; a forged one is rejected.
  • The server sends each player only their own cards — nobody else's hand is ever

transmitted to your device, so a modified app cannot reveal it.

  • We never receive or store passwords.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note it in the

app's release notes. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated

policy.
