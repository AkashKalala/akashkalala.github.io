Privacy Policy — Tenfold

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Tenfold is published by Akash Kalala ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

Tenfold has no database. We do not build a profile of you, we do not sell anything about you, and there are no advertising trackers in the app.

There is one exception to "we collect nothing", and we would rather state it up front than bury it: if the app crashes, we send a crash report. Details in "Crash reports" below, including how to turn it off.

What we collect

If you play offline against the computer: nothing. No account, no network, no data leaves your device.

If you play online, we handle:

Data Why How long
Display name So other players can see whose turn it is In server memory while you are in a room
An account identifier To give you the same seat back if you disconnect In server memory while you are in a room
Your cards and score in the current hand To run the game Deleted when the hand ends
Profile picture (Google sign-in only) Shown next to your name Not stored on our servers

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a signed token from them containing a user identifier and display name, and (from Google) a profile picture URL. We verify that token, then issue our own session token. We never see or receive your password, and we do not receive your contacts, calendar, photos or any other account data.

Guest accounts are a random identifier generated on the spot. They are tied to your device and are lost if you reinstall.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, we send a report so the fault can be found and fixed. Without it a crash affecting one phone model is invisible to us — we would only learn about it from a review, with nothing to act on.

What a crash report contains: the error and the code path that produced it, your device model, operating system version and the app version.

What it does not contain: your name, your account, your email, your cards, or anything about how you play. We explicitly disable the collection of personal information, and strip the user record and IP address from every report before it is sent.

Reports are processed by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) acting on our behalf, and are retained for 90 days.

Turning it off: Settings → Crash reports → Stop sending crash reports. It takes effect immediately, not at next launch.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising SDKs
  • No analytics, behavioural tracking, session recording or performance profiling — crash reports fire only when something breaks, never during normal play
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No payment information — the app has no purchases

Where it is stored

Your session token is stored on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. On our servers, room and game data lives only in memory and is gone when the room closes or the server restarts. We do not run a database and do not write your data to disk.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share your data.

Third parties involved:

  • Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which receives crash reports unless you turn them off. It is a data processor acting on our instructions and does not use the data for its own purposes.
  • Our server is hosted by a cloud provider, which necessarily processes the network traffic between your device and us.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This removes your account and everything we hold about it, immediately and permanently. If you are mid-game, a computer player finishes the hand for the other players and your name is removed from it.

By email: akashkalala123@gmail.com. We will action it within 30 days.

Because we hold no database, deletion is genuinely complete — there is no backup copy or archive to purge afterwards.

Children

Tenfold is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to processing.

Because we store so little, most of these are immediate: everything we hold about you is visible in the app itself, and deletion is a button in Settings. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

  • India (DPDP Act 2023): you may raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer at the address below.
  • EU/UK (GDPR): our lawful basis is legitimate interest in running the game you asked to play. You may complain to your local supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA): we do not sell personal information.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/WSS. Sign-in tokens are cryptographically verified against Google's and Apple's published public keys before we trust them. We hold no passwords, so there are none to leak.

No system is perfectly secure, but the strongest protection here is structural: we do not keep the data in the first place.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note it in the app's release notes.
