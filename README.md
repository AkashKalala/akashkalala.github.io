# akashkalala.github.io

Static pages served at <https://akashkalala.github.io/>.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `tenfold/` | Support pages for **Tenfold**, a contract-rummy card game |
| `tenfold/privacy/` | Privacy policy |
| `tenfold/delete-account/` | How to delete your account and what happens to your data |

The Tenfold pages are generated, not hand-edited — edit them in the app's own repository
and regenerate, so the published policy cannot drift from the reviewed one.
